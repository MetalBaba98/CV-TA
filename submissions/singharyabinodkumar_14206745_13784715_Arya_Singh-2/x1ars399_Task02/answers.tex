    \begin{center}
    \begin{tabular}{lcc}

    \hline
    \end{tabular}

The bottom edge corresponds to the bicycle's wheel-ground contact point, whereas the top of the box (approx 1 m higher, at handlebar height) lies on a different plane, making the cross-ratio invalid there and introducing systematic bias. This also tells us that near the vanishing point, the pixel-to-distance mapping is highly nonlinear, so small pixel errors amplify substantially.
    
    Using the correct reference point (bottom), the bicycle is $\approx 33.54$ m from MB, giving a final distance of $\approx 105$ m from the bicycle to the arch.
